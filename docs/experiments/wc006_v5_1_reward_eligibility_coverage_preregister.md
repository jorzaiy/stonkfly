# WC006 V5.1 reward-eligibility coverage audit

Status: immutable post-failure, non-authorizing diagnostic protocol.

This audit is not a market replay, training run, trading authorization, or
profit test. It asks whether the fixed 2023 sensory input produces reward-linked
eligibility at all observation dates after the fixed cue produced zero reward
eligibility in the mechanism assay.

## Frozen inputs and scope

The audit uses the annual 2023 draft candidate manifest, its hash-pinned
calendar and cache files, and exactly the 242 observation sessions from
2023-01-03 through 2023-12-29. The 17 selector/signal dates remain marked in
the output; no non-selector observation is removed. The active basket changes
only on those 17 frozen selector dates and is carried forward to later dates.

Frames are generated by the existing V5/V4 adapter `_frame` function from the
as-of candidate basket, pinned OHLCV cache, calendar, clean cash/no-holdings
ledger state, multiplier `1.0`, and drawdown `0`. The audit must not substitute
a hand-built cue, choose a date, tune a threshold, or select dates based on
eligibility results.

The controller starts from one clean V5.1 brain checkpoint and processes dates
in chronological order. For each date it calls exactly
`V51Controller.observe(frame, "none")`. No `reinforce_linked`, reward pulse,
aversive pulse, market settlement, order, broker, or execution operation is
allowed. `observe(..., "none")` is the sensory-only V5.1 path; its
`learning=False` propagation may update eligibility traces but does not deliver
a plasticity reinforcement pulse.

## Required daily record

Each append-only event records date, candidate-day flag, active candidate epoch
and codes, frame hash, neural input/spike hashes, KC spike count, reward and
aversive cell spike counts, reward/aversive cell count vectors, and reward and
aversive eligible edge counts immediately after the sensory-only observation.
The event also records `reinforcement="none"`, `market_execution=false`, and
`reinforcement_delivered=false`.

The run records the annual manifest, source/cache, protocol, adapter, V4
adapter, V5.1 adapter, graph/configuration, and clean start/end checkpoint
hashes. Events form a canonical SHA-256 hash chain and are verified before the
summary is written.

## Interpretation

The primary descriptive result is the number of dates and selector dates with
at least one eligible reward-linked edge. `ZERO_ALL_242` means the fixed
annual sensory stream did not produce a reward-linked eligible edge on any
observation date; it is not evidence of profitable or unprofitable learning.
Any error, hash mismatch, missing date, unexpected reinforcement, or input
provenance mismatch fails closed.
